RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

1. PARTIES AND PREMISES.
This Residential Lease Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of January 1, 2026, by and between Horizon Property Holdings ("Landlord") and John Doe ("Tenant") for the leased premises located at 742 Evergreen Terrace, Unit 4B, Springfield ("Premises").

2. TERM OF LEASE.
Section 2.1 Commencement and Expiration: The term of this Lease shall commence on January 1, 2026 and shall expire on December 31, 2026.
Section 2.2 Automatic Renewal: Upon expiration of the initial term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive periods of one (1) year, unless Tenant provides written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days prior to the expiration date.
Section 2.3 Early Termination: If Tenant vacates or terminates prior to the expiration date, Tenant shall be subject to an early termination penalty equal to three (3) months rent or the remaining balance of the lease term.

3. RENT AND SECURITY DEPOSIT.
Section 3.1 Monthly Rent: Tenant agrees to pay base rent of $2,500.00 per month, due on or before the 1st day of each calendar month.
Section 3.2 Security Deposit: Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the sum of $5,000.00 as security deposit. Landlord shall return the security deposit within sixty (60) days following move-out inspection.
Section 3.3 Late Charges: If rent is not received by Landlord by the 5th day of the month, Tenant shall pay a late fee equal to 10% of the monthly rent.

4. RENT ESCALATION.
Section 4.1 Annual Rent Increase: Landlord reserves the unilateral right to increase monthly rent by 8% annually upon providing thirty (30) days advance notice to Tenant.

5. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS.
Section 5.1 Habitability and Upkeep: Tenant shall be solely responsible for all maintenance, plumbing blockages, electrical repairs, and appliance replacements exceeding $100.00 per occurrence.

6. LANDLORD ACCESS.
Section 6.1 Right of Entry: Landlord and Landlord's agents shall have the right to enter the Premises at any time without prior notice for purposes of inspection, maintenance, or showing the unit to prospective tenants.

7. INDEMNIFICATION AND LIABILITY.
Section 7.1 Unlimited Liability: Tenant agrees to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Landlord from any and all damages, accidents, claims, or losses occurring on the Premises with unlimited liability.
